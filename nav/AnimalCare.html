Healthy Weight

A healthy weight is the key to your pet's health and longevity. Establish feeding routines with measured portions, encourage daily exercise through play or walks, and avoid over-rewarding with treats.

Breed-Specific Needs

Every breed has unique health requirements. It is important to research the genetic predispositions of each breed and adapt their diet and activity level to their biological needs.

Specific Nutrition

Feeding should not be left to chance. Look for science-based nutrition that meets the exact requirements of your pet according to their age (puppy, adult, senior), size, and health status.

The Importance of the Veterinarian

If you have any questions, changes in behavior, or for preventive check-ups, always consult a veterinarian. They are the only ones qualified to give a diagnosis and recommend a specific plan.

Animal Abuse

Animal abuse is any action or omission that causes pain, suffering, or harm to an animal. It is a social and public health issue that requires our attention and action.

Forms of Animal Abuse

Physical abuse

Direct actions that cause bodily injury, pain, torture, or the death of the animal.

Neglect and omission of care

Depriving the animal of food, water, adequate shelter from the weather, or veterinary medical attention for illnesses or injuries.

Abandonment and overcrowding

Leaving animals to fend for themselves on public streets, rooftops, or keeping an excessive number of them in small, unsanitary spaces.

Exploitation and sexual abuse

Using animals for sexual practices or forcing them to participate in illegal fights.

How to Report in Tijuana

  • Online: Complete the form on the Citizen Complaint page of the Secretariat of Environment and Sustainable Development of Baja California, which requires a detailed description, type of animal, and at least one photograph as evidence.
  • Legal action: Animal abuse is considered a crime or administrative offense in Mexico, and state prosecutor's offices investigate severe cases that result in serious injury or death of the animal.

Remember: Animals feel, suffer, and deserve a dignified life. Do not be indifferent. If you know of a case of abuse, speak up. Empathy and respect for life are the foundation of a better society.
